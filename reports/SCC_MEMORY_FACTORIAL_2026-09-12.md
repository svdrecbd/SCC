# SCC fractional-history follow-up

The previous construction round is complete and has no positive SCC result.
Its strongest matched delay signal was fractional order 0.85: sustained selected
violation began after 5 ordinary-model updates versus 414 coupled-model updates,
while substantial cognition remained. That observation motivates a controlled
follow-up, not a claim of destructive coupling. The prior result also changed
both history weighting and increment scale, so it cannot isolate their effects.

The new [frozen protocol](../protocols/SCC_MEMORY_FACTORIAL_V1.md) crosses history
order 1/0.85 with increment coefficient 0.5/approximately 0.5176368667. Each of
the four configurations has ordinary and coupled training at three new seeds:
17, 41 and 59. The 24 planned comparisons retain six computational passes, the
same parameter count, identical initial tensors within each seed and the same
20,000 ordinary sample opportunities. Coupled arms add 800 episodes of the
existing objective. They do not receive equal FLOPs or retrospectively matched
gradient magnitudes.

The factorial design addresses whether explicit history helps beyond an ordinary
leaky recurrence with the same increment coefficient. It does not compare every
smoothing kernel or continuous fractional solver. Mathematical motivation and
the original measurement remain in the [construction review](SCC_CONSTRUCTION_SEARCH_2026-09-12.md)
and [independently rescored timing readout](../artifacts/scc-construction-status-20260912T085740Z/READOUT.md).

Qualified models face the previous core/all/benign procedures, a second core
modification stream, a larger learning rate on that same second stream, and
capability-preserving projected directions. Each has bounded external repair
with protection checked again. History-only and coefficient-only graph changes
are first tested for intact competence; qualifying substitutions receive their
own modification/repair challenge. Complete bottleneck bypass is also measured.
These are finite adaptive probes, not exhaustive robustness verification.

Every sampled modification boundary records isolated payload disclosure,
sustained selected violations and retained task/text ability. A 500-step search
without a trigger is censored, not infinitely resistant. Update counts are
reported alongside available procedure timings, and projection receives a
separate 32-update budget rather than being presented as equal compute.
The repair phase is external targeted/replay/refusal adaptation, not autonomous
repair or benign-only rehabilitation.

The two historical conditions reproduce the old model's outputs exactly in the
CPU correspondence checks. Independent scalar-history calculations verify the
four recurrences. Full smooth-objective derivative reruns pass for all four,
and legacy stock paths remain unchanged under the default edit settings. The
full test suite passes 229 tests. Six deliberately unqualified CPU fixtures
complete all paths; independent scoring checks 27,200 endpoint predictions and
10,488 path predictions, across 120 endpoint measurements and 48 curves, together
with source hashes, matched sample schedules and noneditable tensors.
