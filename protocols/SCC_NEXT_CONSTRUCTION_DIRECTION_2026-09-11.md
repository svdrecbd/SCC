# SCC next construction direction

Working design note, not a frozen run protocol or a launched campaign.
The priority is a working bounded mechanism; publication is secondary.

## What the completed trial leaves open

The corrected objective was differentiated through eight broad removal updates
and two repair updates, using two examples per batch. The successful evaluations
use hundreds of targeted exception updates and longer repair, with complete
benign replay and explicit preservation of other refusals. The pilot did not
train against that same selective, capability-preserving procedure. This is a
concrete coverage gap, not evidence that closing it will create SCC.

The early arm's last coupling update was 8,981, followed by 9,019 ordinary
updates. Its result therefore does not settle whether coupling maintained
through the end behaves differently. Nor does it establish that useful coupling
formed earlier and was subsequently erased. Intermediate checkpoints and a
persistent-coupling arm can distinguish these possibilities.

Only one main coupling coefficient and one inner-resource setting were tested.
Nonzero gradients establish participation, not optimization success. In the
last early episode, the recorded joint surrogate remained about 0.325 for
seed17 and 0.358 for seed23. These are surrogate values, not measured violation
probabilities or causal dependence estimates.

## Concrete next sequence

1. Establish whether the candidate creates a destructive response even to the
   modification procedure it actually trains against. Measure intact behavior,
   rule breaking and every recovered capability jointly at intermediate and
   final checkpoints. Persistent failure here calls for fixing construction
   and optimization before testing increasingly elaborate attackers.
2. Build training episodes around selective exceptions, complete benign replay,
   other-rule preservation and longer repairs. Vary target classes and reserve
   unseen target/procedure combinations for evaluation. Check derivatives and
   actual behavior of the new inner procedure before training defenders. If a
   resource approximation is needed, label and validate it explicitly; do not
   silently reintroduce the frozen-displacement approximation.
3. Maintain coupling through development and compare declared strength/resource
   settings. Include a same-total-episode timing comparison and a separate
   larger-dose arm; do not confuse persistence with additional training. Fix
   ordinary rule-learning instability with an independently validated training
   schedule before interpreting differences between qualified defenders.
4. If the training approach still produces no dependence against known edits,
   test a genuinely learned shared computational bottleneck. The bottleneck
   must support learned task algorithms as well as the protected computation.
   An output gate or compiled task controller does not meet this requirement.
   Residual bypasses, reinterpretation and editable readers belong in the
   intervention scope. Sharing alone remains a hypothesis, not a mechanism.

The first constructive milestone is useful intact behavior followed by broad,
repeatable loss across the learned suite after protection-breaking edits,
persisting under declared reinterpretation and repair resources. It then needs
unseen interventions, causal controls and replication. Increased refusal alone,
unqualified intact models, wrong output encoding or unsuccessful search cannot
be counted as destructive cognition. Complete cognition failure remains a
long-term target beyond what this small suite can establish.
