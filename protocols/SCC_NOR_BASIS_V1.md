# SCC stricter basis and editable connection signs — V1

This follow-up is specified after the NAND engineering result and before NOR
training/evaluation. It tests a stronger candidate and an interpretation boundary
that a positive result must survive. It is open finite construction work.

## Construction

Fit NOR `[1,0,0,0]` with the same five-parameter primitive, complete-table BCE,
Adam .05, margin >5, max 1000 updates, seeds 11/29/47. The protected function
accepts `(0,0)` and rejects `(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)`. Compute arithmetic using NAND
programs expanded through synthesized NOR witnesses, within the same program
capacity (four times the canonical NAND instruction count) present before edits.

Uniform false acceptance is the mean output on all three rejected inputs.
For the removal trigger report >=95% false acceptance without requiring benign
acceptance to remain high; breaking benign permission behavior must not conceal
a surviving cognitive capability. Report benign acceptance separately. Also
evaluate a fully supported skewed rejected-input distribution (.48,.04,.48).
No assertion is made that a real alignment task follows these four inputs.

## Interpretation boundaries and counterexamples

The unsigned circuit language is the original V1 language: variables,
constants, shared gates, positive wiring, direct outputs and terminal inversion.
When all three forbidden NOR inputs are accepted, the replacement is OR or a
constant. Check their full finite composition closure, not just original scores.

The signed circuit language includes a sign for each incoming connection from
the intact state. A negative sign supplies the complement of that wire. All
parent signs are positive, but editing them is allowed; adding this operation
only after damage would be an invalid same-boundary repair. Connection signs
are an explicit interpretation capability, not a new learned primitive or an
external destruction mechanism. Parameter counts for the primitive do not
include program/wiring/sign entries; report their modifications separately.

Predeclare these cases: clean; NOR-to-OR shared-gain sign edit under the old
wiring; the same gain edit with signed connections and rewiring; a two-logit
NOR-to-implication edit under old wiring; implication with unsigned synthesized
NAND wiring; and constant-allow under old wiring. OR with both inputs
complemented equals NAND. Verify this local identity independently, then verify
the complete byte tasks. The signed repair may use fewer active slots but must
retain the parent's allocated capacity. No claim of a one-parameter complete
repair: the one-scalar primitive edit also needs program/sign edits.

## Evaluation and exact checks

Reuse and hash the completed NAND study's finite input grids. Exhaust byte XOR,
addition, multiplication, selection and copy. Train fresh NOR parents; qualify
all tasks at 100% before interpreting edits. Do not treat these as independent
neural abilities or an evaluation of a natural-language model.

Enumerate all 16 replacement gates for every unsigned functionally complete
binary parent. Classify uniform refusal removal, benign acceptance and remaining
NAND synthesizability. For the NOR replacements, certify fixed-point closure
both with and without editable connection signs; save constructive witnesses.
Closure concerns finite Boolean functions and the specified operation sets.

Use independent scalar gate evaluation, Boolean witness verification, independent
NumPy full-population circuit replay, integer task oracles, source/data/checkpoint
hashes, and a reduced-width engineering run. Preserve all outputs and failures.
This study cannot establish complete cognition loss: raw copying remains
available, and neural-network mixing/nonlinearities may exceed either language.
CPU only, no new provider charges. SCC remains unestablished unless the actual
