SCC — Safety-Capability Coupling
Research program draft v0.1
Date: 2026-09-09

Primary objective:
Engineer and measure a fail-closed model property in which successful removal of a protected invariant causes disproportionate degradation of indispensable general capability.

Initial scale:
~300M parameters from random initialization.

Scaling ladder:
Tier 0: 20–80M infrastructure validation
Tier 1: 100–400M mechanism demonstration
Tier 2: ~0.7–1.5B serious robustness test
Tier 3: ~2.5–4B scaling/redundancy study
Tier 4: ~6–8B sub-10B capstone

Primary metrics:
- protected-property score S
- general capability C
- escape cost E
- recovery barrier R
- benign editability B
- capability/invariant Pareto frontier

Core falsifier:
Adaptive white-box attackers can repeatedly produce high-capability / low-invariant derivatives at costs comparable to ordinary controls.
